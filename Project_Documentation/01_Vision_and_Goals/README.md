# Project Title: NeuroFocus - Cyberpunk Pomodoro Suite

## Project Vision
Our vision is to revolutionize personal productivity by merging advanced focus techniques with immersive, cyberpunk-inspired aesthetics. NeuroFocus connects the user's workflow with a deeply engaging, sensory-rich environment that utilizes AI, binaural frequencies, and integrated music services to induce and sustain flow states. We aim to move beyond simple time tracking to create a holistic "Focus Operation System" for the modern digital worker.

## Project Goals
- **Enhance Focus Duration**: Increase average user deep work session length by 20% through auditory and visual entrainment (SMART: Measurable via app analytics within 3 months).
- **Seamless Integration**: Achieve < 2s latency for AI commands and Spotify/Music control interactions (SMART: Performance benchmarks).
- **Immersive UX**: Deliver a fully responsive, glitch-free Cyberpunk UI that maintains 60fps on standard hardware, providing a premium feel (SMART: Performance & User Feedback).
- **Cross-Platform Availability**: Ensure full functionality across macOS, Windows, and Linux via Tauri by Q3 2026.
- **Mental Wellness**: Integrate at least 5 configurable binaural beat frequencies specifically targeted at reducing anxiety and promoting concentration.

## Target Audience
- **Developers & Designers**: Professionals who appreciate high-fidelity aesthetics and spend long hours at screens.
- **Neurodivergent Users**: Individuals (ADHD/Autism) who benefit from sensory control, body doubling (via AI Coach), and gamified productivity structure.
- **Students & Researchers**: Academic users requiring sustained periods of concentration without distraction.
- **Sci-Fi Enthusiasts**: Users drawn to the cyberpunk/synthwave aesthetic who find traditional productivity tools sterile and uninspiring.

## Success Metrics
- **Daily Active Users (DAU) / Monthly Active Users (MAU)** ratio > 20%.
- **Retention Rate**: > 40% of users completing at least one Pomodoro session per week after 30 days.
- **Feature Usage**: > 50% of users engaging with AI Coach or Frequency Generator features.
- **Performance**: < 1% crash rate and sub-1s load times for core timer functions.
